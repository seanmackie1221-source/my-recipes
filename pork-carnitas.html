Pork Carnitas Bowls

Serves 4 · Prep 15 min · Cook 3 hrs

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs pork shoulder, cut into large chunks
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 4 cloves garlic, smashed
  • 1 orange, juiced (peel reserved)
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 1 cup chicken or beef broth
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup pico de gallo
  • 1 avocado, sliced

Directions

  1. Season pork chunks with salt, pepper, cumin, and oregano.
  2. Place pork in a slow cooker or Dutch oven with onion, garlic, orange juice and peel, lime juice, broth, and bay leaves.
  3. Slow cooker: cook on low for 8 hours, or high for 4 to 5 hours. Dutch oven: cover and braise at 300F for 3 to 3.5 hours, until pork shreds easily with a fork.
  4. Remove pork, discard bay leaves and orange peel, and shred the meat with two forks.
  5. For crispy edges, spread shredded pork on a baking sheet, drizzle with a little of the cooking liquid, and broil for 5 to 8 minutes until edges crisp up.
  6. Serve carnitas over rice with black beans, pico de gallo, and avocado.
