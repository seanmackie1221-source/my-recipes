Baked Ziti with Italian Sausage

Serves 6 · Prep 20 min · Cook 35 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ziti pasta
  • 1 lb Italian sausage, casings removed
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce
  • 2 cups fresh spinach, chopped
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella, divided
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 1 tsp dried Italian herbs
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375F. Cook ziti in salted boiling water until just under al dente. Drain.
  2. In a large skillet, brown the Italian sausage over medium heat, breaking it apart, about 6 minutes. Add onion and garlic, cooking until softened, about 4 minutes.
  3. Stir in marinara sauce and spinach, and simmer for 5 minutes until spinach wilts.
  4. In a bowl, mix ricotta, egg, 1 cup mozzarella, parmesan, Italian herbs, salt, and pepper.
  5. Combine cooked ziti with the sausage-marinara sauce. Fold in half the ricotta mixture, then transfer to a baking dish. Dollop remaining ricotta mixture on top and sprinkle with remaining mozzarella.
  6. Bake uncovered for 25 to 30 minutes, until bubbly and golden on top.
