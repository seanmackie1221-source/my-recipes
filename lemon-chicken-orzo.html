Lemon-Garlic Chicken Thighs with Orzo

Serves 4 · Prep 10 min · Cook 30 min

Ingredients

  • 6 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 tbsp butter, divided
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1.5 cups orzo pasta
  • 2 cups fresh spinach
  • 1/3 cup grated parmesan
  • 1 lb asparagus, trimmed
  • 1 tbsp olive oil (for asparagus)

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400F. Toss asparagus with olive oil, salt, and pepper, and spread on a baking sheet. Roast for 12 to 15 minutes.
  2. Season chicken thighs with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear chicken for 5 to 6 minutes per side until golden and cooked through. Remove and set aside.
  3. In the same skillet, melt 1 tablespoon butter and add garlic, cooking for 30 seconds until fragrant. Add chicken broth and lemon juice, scraping up browned bits. Simmer for 3 minutes, then stir in remaining butter.
  4. Meanwhile, cook orzo according to package directions. Drain, then toss with spinach until wilted, and stir in parmesan.
  5. Return chicken to the skillet to coat in the lemon-butter sauce. Serve over the orzo with roasted asparagus on the side.
