Korean Beef Bulgogi Bowls

Serves 4 · Prep 20 min · Cook 15 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs thinly sliced beef (ribeye or sirloin)
  • 1/3 cup soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 green onions, chopped
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 1 pear or apple, grated (optional, for tenderizing)
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 4 cups fresh spinach
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 clove garlic, minced (for spinach)

Directions

  1. In a bowl, combine soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, green onions, sesame seeds, and grated pear. Add sliced beef and marinate at least 30 minutes, up to 24 hours in the fridge.
  2. For the quick pickle, toss cucumber slices with rice vinegar, sugar, and salt. Let sit at least 10 minutes.
  3. Heat vegetable oil in a pan over medium heat. Add minced garlic and spinach, and sauté until just wilted, about 2 minutes. Season with a pinch of salt.
  4. Heat a large skillet or grill pan over high heat. Cook marinated beef in batches for 2 to 3 minutes per side, until caramelized and cooked through.
  5. Serve beef over rice with sautéed spinach and pickled cucumber on the side.
