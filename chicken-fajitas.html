Chicken Fajita Bowls

Serves 4 · Prep 15 min · Cook 20 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken breast, sliced thin
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup pico de gallo (tomato, onion, cilantro, lime)
  • 1 avocado, sliced

Directions

  1. In a bowl, toss sliced chicken with 1 tablespoon olive oil, chili powder, cumin, paprika, lime juice, salt, and pepper.
  2. Heat remaining olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add peppers and onion, and cook until softened and slightly charred, about 6 to 8 minutes. Remove and set aside.
  3. In the same skillet, cook the seasoned chicken for 6 to 8 minutes, until cooked through and lightly browned.
  4. Warm the black beans in a small pot or microwave.
  5. Assemble bowls with rice, chicken, peppers and onions, black beans, pico de gallo, and avocado slices.
