Beef and Black Bean Chili

Serves 6 · Prep 15 min · Cook 1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs ground beef
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 1 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese, for serving
  • 1 avocado, sliced, for serving
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped, for serving

Directions

  1. In a large pot over medium-high heat, brown the ground beef, breaking it apart, about 6 to 8 minutes. Drain excess fat if needed.
  2. Add onion, bell pepper, and garlic. Cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in chili powder, smoked paprika, cumin, and cayenne. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Add crushed tomatoes, black beans, and beef broth. Bring to a simmer.
  5. Reduce heat to low and simmer uncovered for 40 to 45 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened.
  6. Stir in dark chocolate until melted. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  7. Serve topped with shredded cheddar, avocado slices, and fresh cilantro.
