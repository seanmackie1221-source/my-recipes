Sheet-Pan Greek Chicken

Serves 4 · Prep 15 min · Cook 40 min

Ingredients

  • 6 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • 1.5 lbs baby potatoes, halved
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup kalamata olives, pitted
  • 1/3 cup olive oil
  • 2 lemons, juiced (zest reserved)
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese
  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1/2 cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
  • 1 clove garlic, minced (for tzatziki)
  • 1 tbsp fresh dill or mint, chopped
  • 1 tbsp olive oil (for tzatziki)

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425F. In a small bowl, whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  2. On a large baking sheet, toss potatoes with half the lemon-oregano mixture. Arrange chicken thighs on top and brush with the remaining mixture.
  3. Roast for 25 minutes, then add cherry tomatoes and olives to the pan. Continue roasting for 15 to 20 minutes, until chicken reaches 165F and potatoes are tender.
  4. While the chicken roasts, make the tzatziki: mix Greek yogurt, grated cucumber, minced garlic, dill, olive oil, and a pinch of salt in a bowl. Refrigerate until ready to serve.
  5. Sprinkle finished chicken and vegetables with crumbled feta and lemon zest. Serve with tzatziki on the side.
