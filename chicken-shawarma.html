Chicken Shawarma Bowls

Serves 4 · Prep 15 min · Cook 20 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced, divided
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice (for sauce)
  • 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

Directions

  1. In a bowl, combine 2 tablespoons olive oil, turmeric, cumin, paprika, cinnamon, 3 cloves minced garlic, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Add chicken thighs and marinate at least 30 minutes, up to 24 hours.
  2. Heat remaining olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook chicken thighs for 6 to 7 minutes per side, until browned and cooked through. Rest, then slice.
  3. For the salad, toss cucumber and cherry tomatoes with a pinch of salt.
  4. For the sauce, mix yogurt, remaining garlic clove, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt.
  5. Assemble bowls with rice, sliced chicken, cucumber-tomato salad, garlic sauce, and fresh parsley.
