Beef Stroganoff

Serves 4 · Prep 15 min · Cook 25 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs sirloin steak, thinly sliced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 3 tbsp butter, divided
  • 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 2 cups beef broth
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 3/4 cup sour cream
  • 12 oz egg noodles or 2 cups cooked rice
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

Directions

  1. Season sliced beef with salt and pepper. Heat 1 tablespoon butter in a large skillet over high heat. Sear beef in batches for 1 to 2 minutes per side until browned. Remove and set aside.
  2. Add remaining butter to the pan. Add mushrooms and onion, cooking until softened and browned, about 6 to 8 minutes. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Sprinkle flour over the vegetables and stir to coat. Cook for 1 minute, then gradually whisk in beef broth. Simmer until thickened, about 4 minutes.
  4. Stir in Dijon mustard and sour cream. Return beef to the pan and warm through, without boiling.
  5. Meanwhile, cook egg noodles according to package directions if using. Drain.
  6. Serve stroganoff over egg noodles or rice, topped with fresh parsley.
