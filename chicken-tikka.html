Chicken Tikka Masala

Serves 4 · Prep 20 min · Cook 30 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cubed
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tsp garam masala, divided
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp butter or ghee
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 cups cauliflower florets or 1.5 cups frozen peas
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped

Directions

  1. In a bowl, combine yogurt, lemon juice, 1 teaspoon garam masala, cumin, turmeric, and salt. Add chicken and marinate at least 30 minutes, up to overnight.
  2. Heat butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion and cook until softened, about 4 minutes. Add garlic and ginger, cooking for 1 minute.
  3. Add marinated chicken and cook for 5 to 6 minutes, until lightly browned on the outside.
  4. Stir in crushed tomatoes and remaining garam masala. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Add cauliflower florets and cook for 10 to 12 minutes until tender, or stir in peas during the last 3 minutes if using.
  6. Stir in heavy cream and simmer for 5 more minutes. Season with salt to taste.
  7. Serve over rice, topped with fresh cilantro.
