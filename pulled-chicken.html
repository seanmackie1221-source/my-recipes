Slow Cooker Pulled BBQ Chicken

Serves 6 · Prep 10 min · Cook 6 hrs

Ingredients

  • 2.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts
  • 1.5 cups BBQ sauce, divided
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 4 cups coleslaw mix
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar (for slaw)
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 cups cooked rice or roasted potatoes, for serving

Directions

  1. Place chicken in the slow cooker. Add 1 cup BBQ sauce, chicken broth, apple cider vinegar, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.
  2. Cover and cook on low for 6 hours, or high for 3 to 4 hours, until chicken shreds easily.
  3. Remove chicken and shred with two forks. Return to the slow cooker and stir in remaining BBQ sauce.
  4. For the coleslaw, toss coleslaw mix with mayonnaise, apple cider vinegar, sugar, salt, and pepper.
  5. Serve pulled chicken over rice or with roasted potatoes, topped with coleslaw.
