Beef and Broccoli Stir-Fry

Serves 4 · Prep 15 min · Cook 15 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs flank steak, thinly sliced against the grain
  • 1/3 cup soy sauce, divided
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch, divided
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil, divided
  • 4 cups broccoli florets
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup beef broth
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds, for garnish

Directions

  1. Toss sliced beef with 2 tablespoons soy sauce and 1 tablespoon cornstarch. Let sit for 15 minutes.
  2. Heat 1/2 tablespoon vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat. Add broccoli and stir-fry for 3 to 4 minutes until bright green and crisp-tender. Remove and set aside.
  3. Add remaining oil to the pan. Add beef in a single layer and sear for 1 to 2 minutes per side until browned. Remove and set aside.
  4. Add garlic and ginger to the pan, cooking for 30 seconds. Whisk together remaining soy sauce, remaining cornstarch, brown sugar, and beef broth, then pour into the pan. Simmer until thickened, about 2 minutes.
  5. Return beef and broccoli to the pan, tossing to coat in the sauce. Stir in sesame oil.
  6. Serve over rice, garnished with sesame seeds.
