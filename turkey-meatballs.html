Turkey Meatballs in Marinara

Serves 4 · Prep 20 min · Cook 30 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs ground turkey
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce
  • 2 zucchini, sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cups cooked polenta or rice

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400F. In a bowl, combine ground turkey, breadcrumbs, parmesan, egg, garlic, parsley, salt, and pepper. Mix gently and form into 16 to 18 meatballs.
  2. Place meatballs on a baking sheet and bake for 15 minutes, until lightly browned.
  3. Meanwhile, toss sliced zucchini with olive oil, salt, and pepper, and roast on a separate sheet for 15 to 18 minutes.
  4. Heat marinara sauce in a large pot or deep skillet over medium heat. Add baked meatballs and simmer for 10 minutes, until cooked through and coated in sauce.
  5. Serve meatballs and sauce over polenta or rice, with roasted zucchini on the side.
