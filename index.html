Moroccan-Spiced Chicken Thighs with Saffron-Turmeric Rice

Serves 4 · Prep 15 min · Cook 35 min

Ingredients

  • 6 bone-in skin-on chicken thighs
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced, divided
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 head cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 1.5 cups basmati rice
  • 1/4 tsp saffron threads
  • 3 cups chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp fresh cilantro or mint, chopped

Directions

  1. Combine cumin, cinnamon, paprika, ginger, turmeric, 3 cloves minced garlic, 2 tbsp olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper in a bowl. Rub over chicken thighs and marinate at least 30 minutes or up to 2 days in the fridge.
  2. Preheat oven to 425F. Toss cauliflower florets with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper, then spread on a baking sheet.
  3. Place marinated chicken thighs skin-side up on a separate baking sheet. Roast chicken and cauliflower together for 30 to 35 minutes, until chicken reaches 165F internal temperature and cauliflower is golden.
  4. While chicken roasts, rinse rice, then bring chicken broth and saffron to a simmer in a pot. Add rice, cover, and cook on low for 15 to 18 minutes until liquid is absorbed.
  5. Mix yogurt, remaining garlic clove grated, chopped herbs, salt, and pepper for the sauce.
  6. Serve chicken and cauliflower over rice, topped with yogurt sauce.

Freezer note: Marinate raw chicken and freeze in the marinade for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in fridge before roasting.
