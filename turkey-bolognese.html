Turkey and Mushroom Bolognese

Serves 4 · Prep 15 min · Cook 35 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground turkey
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 8 oz mushrooms, finely diced
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1 zucchini, grated
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 lb pasta of choice
  • Grated parmesan, for serving

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion and garlic, and cook until softened, about 3 minutes.
  2. Add ground turkey and cook, breaking it apart, until no longer pink, about 5 to 7 minutes.
  3. Stir in mushrooms, carrot, and zucchini. Cook for another 5 minutes until vegetables soften and release their liquid.
  4. Add crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, oregano, basil, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine.
  5. Reduce heat to low and simmer uncovered for 20 to 25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until sauce thickens.
  6. Meanwhile, cook pasta according to package directions. Drain.
  7. Serve bolognese over pasta, topped with grated parmesan.
